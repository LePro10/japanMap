# Neon Basin city implementation plan

> For agentic workers: use superpowers:subagent-driven-development for isolated implementation tasks and review; integrate and verify in agent/neon-city.

**Goal:** Turn the existing city into a richly dressed, explorable Japanese city with distinct districts, a landmark crossing, walk-in shops, a garden and consistent quality scaling.

**Architecture:** Keep the WP6 road and terrain contract. Extend the city generator with stable building descriptors and authored reserves. Build batched street architecture, compact interiors and garden/landmark geometry from those descriptors; register collision and walking floors from the same geometry coordinates. Scale incidental geometry by distance and preset while preserving entrances and occupied space.

**Tech stack:** Existing TypeScript, Three.js, Vite, Node geometry tests. No new runtime dependency.

**Spec:** User-approved six-part design in this conversation; ASTRA_PLAN.md sections 3, 7 and 13. The user's current city-first scope overrides the old event-first sequence.

## Constraints

- Work only in isolated lanes; finish using tools/agent-isolate.mjs --finish. Never push.
- Preserve 72-road WP6 world; no terrain regeneration needed for this pass.
- No new traffic/pedestrian work.
- English game copy; metric Y-up coordinates; original signs and identities.
- Keep all walking destinations on every preset; reduce secondary detail and effects.
- Dispose all generated GPU resources. Do not modify assets/generated manually.
